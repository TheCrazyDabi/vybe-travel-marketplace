Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

Welcome to VYBE ("VYBE", "we", "our", or "us"). We provide a travel discovery and booking marketplace connecting travellers with verified tour operators. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our website and Android mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide, secure, and improve our services:

  • Account Details: Full name, email address, password hashes (salted using PBKDF2; we never store plaintext passwords), and account role (traveller or tour operator).
  • Travel & Booking Information: Travel dates, requested party sizes, booking references, communication history between travellers and operators, and package preferences.
  • Operator Verification Documents: Business registration certificates, travel licenses, and identification documents submitted by tour operators. These documents are stored privately in encrypted cloud storage (Cloudflare R2) and accessible only to authorized administrators for compliance verification.
  • Payment Data: Transaction references, Razorpay order IDs, and payment verification timestamps. We never store credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank account credentials on our servers. All payments are tokenized and processed securely through Razorpay.
  • Device & Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device registration tokens to deliver booking status alerts and operator responses.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for legitimate marketplace functions:

  • Processing booking requests, inquiries, and quotation workflows.
  • Preventing fraud and validating operator authenticity before listing packages.
  • Sending transactional push notifications and critical account alerts.
  • Complying with applicable legal, taxation, and anti-fraud statutory obligations.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. We disclose information only to service providers essential for platform operations:

  • Razorpay: Secure payment processing and webhook reconciliation.
  • Cloudflare R2: Private, encrypted storage for operator documentation.
  • Supabase / PostgreSQL: Secure cloud database hosting with row-level security.
  • Google Firebase: Push notification dispatch.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You have the absolute right to request the deletion of your account and personal data at any time:

How to Delete Your Account:
  • In-App: Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  • Web Deletion Portal (No App Required): Submit a request at vybe.travel/account/delete-request.

Upon receiving your request, personal identifiers (name, email) are permanently anonymized, device tokens are revoked, and active sessions are terminated. Immutable financial audit records are retained strictly as required by applicable tax law.

5. Security Safeguards

We enforce strict technical security standards including end-to-end TLS/HTTPS encryption, encrypted session cookies, rate-limiting on authentication endpoints, and strict role-based access control.
